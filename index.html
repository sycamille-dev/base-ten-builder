How to play

  • Add a block: drag it from the block strip up into the chart, or just tap the strip.
  • Remove a block: tap it, or drag it back down to the strip.
  • Exchange: 10 ones make 1 ten, and 10 tens make 1 hundred. Drag a ten into the Exchange box to break it into 10 ones, or use the trade buttons.
  • Check: press Check my answer or the Enter key when you are ready.
  • Stuck? Press Hint, or Undo / Start over.
  • Count the Blocks: the chart is already filled. Count the ten-rods and the one-cubes, then write the total in the boxes. Trading 10 ones for 1 ten keeps the same total, but taking blocks away changes it — so count carefully!
  • Word Problems: read the story, spot the clue words (more = add, left = subtract), write the answer in the column boxes, then build it with blocks.
  • Multiplication: break the numbers into tens and ones, build each part of the array (like 10 × 5 = 50), then add the parts and write the product.
  • Column boxes: start from the ones on the right. When a column needs a carry or a borrow, a small orange box appears above it — write the tiny number there, just like on your worksheet!
  • Prizes: finish any activity to win a candy, toy or squishy — see them on your 🏆 Prize Shelf!
